## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Shadow Dungeon**. This trainer enhances Shadow Dungeon by providing optional control over player survivability, resources, equipment progression, experience, and combat efficiency. Use the features independently to customize the difficulty without changing the core action RPG experience.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with classes, talent trees, equipment builds, campaign progression, and Greater Rift difficulty without repeating extended farming sessions.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **God Mode** — Prevents the player character from losing health during dungeon runs.
- **Unlimited Mana** — Keeps mana available for repeated skill use without normal resource limitations.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **One-Hit Kills** — Defeats standard enemies with a single successful attack.
- **Unlimited Skill Use** — Removes normal skill cooldown restrictions for rapid combat testing.
- **Damage Multiplier** — Increases outgoing damage to speed up fights against monsters and bosses.
- **Freeze Enemies** — Stops affected enemies from acting while the trainer feature is active.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Shadow Dungeon uses real-time movement in its 2.5D dungeon environments. Mouse movement is supported on PC, while controller play includes automatic target acquisition and automatic attacks. Keep movement responsive during combat and avoid activating trainer options while changing input devices or control settings.

### Tools & Inventory

Loot includes weapons, armor, gems, potions, runes, and equipment with varied affixes. Use the trainer's gold, experience, and skill-point options to test builds, then manage equipment normally through the backpack, storage, enhancement, reforging, and enchanting systems.

### World & Environment

The campaign spans seven chapters and more than 160 maps and levels. Four playable characters provide different talent-tree approaches, while enemies, bosses, shrines, loot, and progression systems support repeated dungeon runs. Greater Rifts become available after completing the main story and add higher difficulty tiers with improved rewards.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is a solo, real-time mod-and-slash system built around attacks, skills, cooldowns, potions, enemy patterns, and equipment-based build synergy. Trainer options such as God Mode, unlimited mana, damage multipliers, and frozen enemies are best used for testing skills, studying boss encounters, or reducing repetitive battles.
